SOLMARA

Open-world crime sandbox. Steal a car, lose the heat, pull the Big One.

WASD / arrows — move / drive  ·  Mouse drag — orbit camera

Shift — run / handbrake in a car  ·  Space — jump / handbrake

E — enter, exit or steal a car (hold E at the bank vault in M5)

Q — draw / holster pistol  ·  Left click — shoot / punch  ·  F — punch

R — flip / reset car  ·  Esc / P — pause  ·  M — mute
